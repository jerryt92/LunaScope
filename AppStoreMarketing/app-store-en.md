## Description

Take more control of Insta360 Luna series cameras from your iPhone or iPad. Frame your shot in live view, adjust exposure, steer the gimbal, and capture photos or video.

Save a gimbal position
Use the on-screen joystick to adjust pan and tilt. Save the current gimbal position and return to the last saved angle when you need the same framing again.

Shoot at an interval
Choose an interval from the menu or set your own from 1 second to 60 minutes. The camera takes photos at the interval you select.

Keep your favorite capture settings
Create presets for portraits, night skies, and other scenes. Save settings such as exposure, white balance, and focus together, then tap a preset to apply it.

Check shooting status with Live Activities
See camera connection, video recording, and interval shooting status on the iPhone Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. Tap the Live Activity to return to LunaScope.

Capture features require a connection to an Insta360 Luna series camera.
